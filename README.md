# Ex.No.-1.1-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM

To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.

## REQUIREMENTS

  1. Autodesk fusion 360
  2. Windows 10
  3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 2 GB or more)
  4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload 
  
## PROCEDURE

### PROCEDURE

**1. Opening the Software**

1. First, Autodesk Fusion 360 software is opened on the computer.
2. A new design file is created by selecting the New Design option.
3. The Create Sketch option is selected from the toolbar.
4. The XY plane is selected to start drawing the given mechanical component.

**2. Drawing the Upper Circular Feature**

5. The Circle (C) tool is selected from the Sketch toolbar.
6. An outer circle of diameter Ø32 mm is drawn at the upper portion of the component.
7. Another concentric circle of diameter Ø20 mm is drawn to create the inner circular feature.
8. The required dimensions are applied using the Sketch Dimension (D) tool.

**3. Drawing the Side Circular Features**

9. The Circle tool is selected to draw the left-side circular feature.
10. The outer circle of radius R10 mm and the inner circle of radius R7 mm are drawn according to the given drawing.
11. The right-side circular feature is drawn in the same manner.
12. The horizontal distance of 47 mm is maintained between the two side circular centres.
13. The required dimensions are applied using the Sketch Dimension tool.

**4. Drawing the Bottom Circular Feature**

14. The Circle tool is selected to draw the bottom circular feature at the centre of the component.
15. An outer circle of diameter Ø20 mm and an inner circle of diameter Ø10 mm are drawn.
16. The bottom circular feature is positioned according to the given engineering drawing.
17. The required dimensions are applied to maintain the correct position and size.

**5. Creating the Outer Curved Profile**

18. The Arc tool is selected from the Sketch toolbar.
19. The upper connecting curved profiles are drawn between the top circular feature and the side circular features.
20. The required radius dimensions of R64 mm are applied to the upper side curves.
21. The lower connecting curved profiles are drawn between the two side circular features and the bottom circular feature.
22. The required radius dimensions of R20 mm and R10 mm are applied wherever specified in the drawing.

**6. Creating the Central Decorative Profile**

23. The Line and Arc tools are selected to create the central decorative profile.
24. The required curved portions are drawn around the centre of the component.
25. The radius dimension of R6 mm is applied to the required curved portions.
26. The Trim (T) tool is used to remove unwanted portions and obtain the required central profile.

**7. Applying Dimensions**

27. The Sketch Dimension (D) tool is selected from the toolbar.
28. The required diameter dimensions are applied to all circular features.
29. The specified radius dimensions are applied to the curved profiles.
30. The horizontal and vertical distances are entered according to the given engineering drawing.
31. The overall vertical dimensions of 65 mm, 26 mm and 24 mm are applied and checked.

**8. Modifying the Sketch**

32. The Trim (T) tool is used to remove unwanted lines, arcs and overlapping portions.
33. The Fillet tool is selected to create smooth rounded connections wherever required.
34. Suitable sketch constraints are applied to maintain the required geometrical relationships.
35. The sketch is modified wherever necessary to obtain the correct component profile.

**9. Checking and Measuring the Sketch**

36. The Inspect menu is selected from the toolbar.
37. The Measure option is selected.
38. The required geometrical elements are selected to measure their dimensions.
39. The measured dimensions are compared with the values given in the engineering drawing.
40. Any dimensional errors are corrected using the Sketch Dimension and modification tools.

**10. Completing the Sketch**

41. The completed sketch is checked carefully to ensure that all circular features, curved profiles and dimensions are correct.
42. The Finish Sketch option is selected to complete the 2D sketch.
43. The completed design is saved using the Save option in Autodesk Fusion 360.

**11. Creating the Engineering Drawing**

44. After finishing the sketch, the workspace is switched from Design to Drawing.
45. The Drawing option is selected from the Design menu.
46. The From Design option is selected to create a drawing of the completed component.
47. In the Create Drawing window, Full Assembly is selected under Reference.
48. Under Destination, Create New is selected for the drawing.
49. The template is selected as From Scratch.
50. The drawing standard is set to ISO.
51. The unit is selected as millimetres (mm).
52. The sheet size is selected as A3 (420 mm × 297 mm).
53. The OK button is clicked to create the drawing sheet.

**12. Placing the Drawing View**

54. The drawing workspace opens with the selected drawing sheet.
55. The required view of the completed component is placed on the drawing sheet.
56. The ISO standard is used to obtain the required drawing view.
57. The position and scale of the drawing view are adjusted to fit the sheet properly.
58. The required dimensions are checked to ensure that the drawing represents the completed component correctly.

**13. Saving the Final Drawing**

59. The completed engineering drawing is checked carefully for its shape, dimensions and drawing view.
60. The final drawing is saved in Autodesk Fusion 360 for future reference.
